What Is MarriageGuard?

A different way to think about financial commitment.

MarriageGuard is a proposed financial services concept designed to help engaged and married couples establish a contractual savings arrangement together.

Both partners voluntarily participate, agree to the financial terms, and establish how accumulated savings could be distributed under specified circumstances.

How It Could Work

Two people. One commitment. A clearly defined agreement.

01. Enroll Together

Both partners complete the enrollment process and review the proposed agreement.

02. Establish Savings

Couples would establish an approved savings arrangement through a participating financial institution.

03. Build Together

Both partners contribute toward a combined savings balance under their agreed terms.

04. Defined Outcomes

The agreement would define how savings could be distributed if specified circumstances occur.

A Commitment Worth Protecting.

MarriageGuard is being developed around the idea that financial agreements should be clear, transparent, and thoughtfully designed.
